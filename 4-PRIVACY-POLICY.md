## 1. Introduction

Agrovite ("we," "us," "our") operates a marketplace platform connecting farmers, buyers, and transporters. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using Agrovite, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide directly
- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number, password.
- **Verification information:** depending on the method you choose —
  - **Face ID / biometric data**, if you select facial verification;
  - **Email or phone confirmation** codes/links;
  - **Location and full name**, if you select that verification method.
- **Profile details:** your role (farmer, buyer, transporter), business or farm details you choose to add.
- **Listings and transaction content:** produce listings, prices, quantities, order details, and messages you exchange with other users.
- **Payment information:** processed by our third-party payment provider(s) — see [Section 4](#4-third-party-services).

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Location data:** approximate location, used to show nearby listings/buyers (e.g., distance shown on listings).
- **Device and usage data:** device type, browser, IP address, app interactions, and log data, used for security and improving the service.

### 2.3 Information from others
- Information other users share about a transaction involving you (e.g., an order or message referencing you).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account and verify your identity.
- Connect farmers, buyers, and transporters based on location and listings.
- Facilitate messaging, orders, and secure/escrow-style payments.
- Show relevant price alerts and market information.
- Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or violations of our [Terms of Use](./5-TERMS-OF-USE.md).
- Communicate with you about your account, transactions, or platform updates.
- Improve and develop the Agrovite platform.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We may share information with:
- **Payment processors** (e.g., a licensed Nigerian payment gateway), to process and hold payments in escrow until delivery is confirmed.
- **Verification/KYC providers**, to confirm identity via the method you choose.
- **Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers**, to store and run the platform securely.
- **Analytics providers**, to understand how the app is used (in aggregate/anonymized form where possible).

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.
