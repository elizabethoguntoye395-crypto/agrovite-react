# Agrovite — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is Agrovite?**
Agrovite is a marketplace app that connects farmers directly with buyers and transporters, so produce can be sold and bought without middlemen taking a cut.

**Who is Agrovite for?**
Farmers who want to sell directly, buyers (retailers, wholesalers, or individuals) who want fresh produce at fair prices, and transporters who move goods between the two.

**Is Agrovite free to use?**
Agrovite is currently in early access/beta. Pricing and any platform fees will be communicated clearly before they apply to your account.

**Which regions does Agrovite currently serve?**
Agrovite is starting with select markets and will expand based on demand. Check the app for current coverage in your area.

## Account & Verification

**How do I create an account?**
Tap **Sign up** on the login screen, enter your email or phone number and a password, then follow the verification step. See the [User Guide](./2-USER-GUIDE.md) for a full walkthrough.

**Why do I need to verify my identity?**
Verification helps keep the marketplace trustworthy — it reduces fake listings and protects both farmers and buyers from fraud.

**What verification methods are available?**
Face ID, a confirmation link sent to your email, an SMS code sent to your phone, or confirming your location and full name. You only need to complete one.

**Can I sign in without a password?**
Yes — use **Scan QR code instead** on the sign-in screen and scan the code from the Agrovite mobile app.

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Use the password reset option on the login screen. If you don't see one yet, contact support through your account settings.

**Can I switch between being a farmer and a buyer?**
Yes. Your dashboard has a **Farmer view / Buyer view** toggle, so one account can do both if needed.

## Buying Produce

**How do I find produce near me?**
Use the search bar or browse the **Produce near you** grid on your dashboard. Listings show the crop, seller, distance, and price.

**How do I contact a seller?**
Tap **Message** on any listing to start a direct conversation.

**How does payment work?**
When you place an order, your payment is held securely until the seller delivers and you confirm you've received it — this protects you from paying for something that never arrives.

**What if my order doesn't arrive or isn't as described?**
Since payment is held until you confirm delivery, you should only confirm once you're satisfied. If there's a dispute, contact support before confirming.

**Can I track my order?**
Yes, your orders table shows live status: awaiting pickup, in transit, or delivered.

## Selling Produce

**How do I list my produce?**
Tap **+ Add produce** on your farmer dashboard, fill in the product details, quantity, and price, and save. It's visible to nearby buyers immediately.

**How do I know if my listing is doing well?**
Check your listing's status badge — Active, Low stock, or Sold out — and watch your Messages panel for buyer interest.

**When do I get paid?**
Once you've delivered and the buyer confirms receipt, the held payment is released to you.

**What if a buyer doesn't pay or disappears?**
Because payment is held by Agrovite before delivery, you won't be asked to hand over produce without a confirmed, funded order. Contact support if a buyer isn't communicating after agreeing to an order.

**Can I update my price after listing?**
Yes, edit your listing anytime from your produce list.

## Prices & Alerts

**What are price alerts?**
A panel showing how prices for crops you care about are moving in nearby markets, so you can price fairly or spot a good deal.

**Where do the prices come from?**
Prices reflect recent activity on the platform in each market/location. (In the current beta, sample prices are shown as a preview of this feature.)

## Trust & Safety

**Is it safe to pay through Agrovite?**
Yes — using Agrovite's payment system (rather than paying a seller or transporter directly, outside the app) is the safest option, since your payment is protected until delivery is confirmed.

**How do I report a suspicious listing or user?**
Use the report option on the listing or profile, or contact support through your account settings.

**Does Agrovite check who's on the platform?**
Yes, every account completes an identity verification step before being able to trade.

---

## Technical

**Which devices can I use Agrovite on?**
Agrovite works on both desktop and mobile browsers, and is designed mobile-first for use in the field.

**I found a bug — what should I do?**
Please report it through your account's support/feedback option, describing what happened and what you expected instead.

**Is my data backed up if I lose my phone?**
Your account data lives on Agrovite's servers, not just your device, so logging in from a new device restores your listings, orders, and messages.
