# Agrovite — User Guide

Welcome to Agrovite! This guide walks you through everything you need to buy or sell produce directly, with no middleman.

---

## 1. What is Agrovite?

Agrovite connects **farmers** with **buyers** (retailers, wholesalers, individuals) and **transporters** — directly. You list what you have or want, chat with the other side, agree a price, and get paid or receive your order safely.

---

## 2. Getting Started

### Step 1: Open the app
Go to the Agrovite homepage. You'll see a short pitch, live example prices, and a "Get early access" button.

### Step 2: Create an account
1. Tap **Get early access** or **Join the beta**.
2. Choose **Sign up** at the top of the form.
3. Enter your email or phone number and choose a password.
4. Tap **Create account →**.

Already have an account? Choose **Log in** instead and enter your details.

### Step 3: Verify it's you
After signing in, you'll be asked to pick a verification method:

| Method | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🙂 Face ID | Confirms your identity with a quick face scan |
| ✉️ Link email | Sends a confirmation link to your email |
| 📱 Phone number | Sends a code by SMS |
| 📍 Location & name | Confirms your general area and full name |

Pick whichever is easiest for you, then tap **Verify & continue →**.

> **Prefer to use your phone?** Tap **Scan QR code instead** on the sign-in screen and scan the code with the Agrovite mobile app.

### Step 4: You're in!
Once verified, tap **Go to my dashboard →**. You'll land on your personal dashboard.

## 3. Using Agrovite as a Farmer

Your dashboard opens on **Farmer view** by default (or switch to it using the toggle at the top of the sidebar).

### Your dashboard, at a glance
- **Active listings** — how many produce items you currently have up for sale.
- **Pending orders** — orders waiting for you to confirm or fulfil.
- **This month's earnings** — your running total for the month.
- **Unread messages** — buyers waiting to hear back from you.

### Listing your produce
1. Tap **+ Add produce**.
2. Enter the product name, quantity, unit (basket, bag, unit, crate, etc.), and your price.
3. Add a photo if you can — listings with photos get more attention.
4. Save. Your listing appears immediately to nearby buyers.

Your listings table shows a status for each item:
- 🟢 **Active** — visible and available to buyers.
- 🟠 **Low stock** — you're running low; consider updating quantity.
- 🔴 **Sold out** — no longer visible to new buyers.

### Talking to buyers
Tap into your **Messages** panel to see who's reached out. Reply directly to agree on quantity, price, and delivery — there's no need to go through anyone else.

### Getting paid
Once a buyer places an order, the payment is **held securely** until you've delivered and the buyer confirms receipt. You'll see the order move through:

`Awaiting pickup → In transit → Delivered → Paid`

### Price alerts
Keep an eye on the **Market price alerts** panel to see how prices for crops like yours are moving nearby, so you can price competitively.

## 4. Using Agrovite as a Buyer

Switch to **Buyer view** using the toggle in the sidebar.

### Your dashboard, at a glance
- **Active orders** — what's on its way to you.
- **Saved sellers** — farmers you buy from often.
- **Total spent** — your running total for the month.
- **Unread messages** — sellers waiting to hear from you.

### Finding produce
1. Use the search bar at the top, or scroll the **Produce near you** grid.
2. Each card shows the crop, seller, distance, and price.
3. Tap **Message** on any listing to start a conversation with the seller.

### Placing an order
Agree on quantity and delivery with the seller directly through chat. Once you're both happy, confirm the order — your payment is held safely until the produce is delivered to you.

### Tracking your order
Check the **Your orders** table for live status: **In transit** or **Delivered**.

### Watching prices
Add crops to your **Price watch** panel to track how prices move across different markets, so you always know if you're getting a fair deal.

## 5. General Tips

- **Keep your profile accurate** — buyers and sellers trust listings and profiles with clear, honest details.
- **Use chat for everything** — quantity, price, and delivery agreements should all happen in writing, inside the app, so there's a record if anything needs resolving.
- **Check status badges** before messaging — no point reaching out about a sold-out listing.
- **Don't share payment details outside the app** — Agrovite's secure payment system exists to protect both sides; using it is safer than sending money directly.
